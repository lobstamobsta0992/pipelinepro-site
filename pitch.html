Why Modern Lead Gen is Broken

Most plumbing, electrical, and roofing business owners are incredible at their trade but lose thousands of dollars on marketing. Here are the two primary bottlenecks they encounter daily:

1. The Shared Lead Trap

Lead sites like Angie's List, Yelp, and HomeAdvisor sell the **exact same lead** to 5 or more local contractors at the same moment. It forces you to battle on price, call the client 20 times just to get a busy signal, and burn out your technicians chasing flaky prospects.

Traditional ad agencies charge you a hefty flat monthly retainer fee *plus* ask you to spend thousands in monthly Google or Facebook ad spend. They generate plenty of "clicks" or "form fills," but you are too busy in the field to call them back immediately, causing leads to freeze over and die.
